# SUPERSTORE-DATA-ANALYSIS-
Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance for J.Space Superstores from 2014 to 2017. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, discounts, product performance, regional performance, state-level performance, and yearly profit trends.
An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to transform the sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

## 📌Key Insights
1. Overall Business Performance
The business generated approximately $2.30 million in total sales during the four-year period, with approximately $286,397 in total profit.
This indicates that the business generated substantial revenue while maintaining a positive overall profit position.
2. Product Performance
The dashboard shows that Staple Envelopes recorded the highest quantity among the top five selling products, followed by Staples and Easy-staple Paper.
This suggests that everyday office-supply products contribute significantly to sales volume.
3. Most Profitable Sub-Categories
Copiers were the most profitable sub-category, generating approximately $55.6K in profit.
They were followed by:
Phones — approximately $44.5K
Accessories — approximately $41.9K
Paper — approximately $34.1K
Binders — approximately $30.2K
These categories represent important contributors to overall profitability.
4. Category Quantity
The quantity analysis shows considerable variation between product categories.
Binders recorded the highest quantity at approximately 1,523 units, followed by Paper at approximately 1,370 units.
This shows that high sales volume is concentrated in particular product categories.
5. Regional Performance
The dashboard compares sales across the Central, East, South, and West regions.
The regional analysis shows that sales are not evenly distributed across the business, indicating that some regions contribute more strongly to overall revenue than others.
This provides an opportunity for management to investigate why certain regions perform better and whether successful strategies can be replicated elsewhere.
6. State-Level Performance
The state-level charts show significant differences in both sales and profit.
Some states have noticeably higher peaks than others, indicating that geographical location has an important relationship with business performance.
Further investigation could identify the factors driving stronger performance in these states.
7. Yearly Profit Trend
Profit fluctuated considerably between 2014 and 2017.
The trend shows several periods of sharp increases and decreases rather than consistent growth. This suggests that management should investigate the causes of major changes in profitability, including product mix, discounts, sales volume, and regional performance.

## Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:
Focus on highly profitable sub-categories such as Copiers, Phones, and Accessories while maintaining strong performance in other profitable categories.
Investigate high-performing states to understand the factors contributing to their stronger sales and profitability.
Analyze periods of significant profit decline to determine whether they were caused by reduced sales, increased discounts, changes in product mix, or other operational factors.
Maintain sufficient inventory for high-volume products, particularly products such as Binders and Paper.
Compare regional strategies and identify practices from stronger-performing regions that could be applied to weaker-performing regions.
